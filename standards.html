01The architecture

Why the suite has the shape it does.

The suite is organized on a tensegrity model. Seven compressive standards resist force: they hold their form under adversarial pressure, the way struts hold under load. Three generative standards produce capacity: they carry tension the way cables do, connecting everything and keeping the rigid members in relation. Neither class functions alone, and the architecture documents below derive the model, name the structural patterns it detects, and sequence adoption.

One standard is prior to the rest: the Precision-First Design Standard is the suite's meta-standard, and every other standard's conformance language is held to it.

02The struts

Compressive standards

Seven struts

Precision-First Design Standard

v2.4.3 Read the standard ↗
"This standard addresses precision failure in two directions. A precision deficit occurs when instrument terms are under-specified: violations become undetectable and the gaps remain permanently exploitable. A precision imposition occurs when an instrument is over-specified: compliance can be demonstrated while the coordination purpose remains unserved."Abstract

In brief: the suite's meta-standard. Every term made operational, so a violation is detectable instead of arguable; all nine other standards rest on it.

Adverse-Signal Engagement Principle Core Standard

v0.7.13 Read the standard ↗
"Adverse signals must be treated as primary inputs to coordination and security, not as nuisances to be minimized."Definition, §2.1

In brief: the unwelcome signal treated as a primary input, not noise: a structural obligation to engage it before the failure it predicts arrives.

Structural Consent Legibility Standard

v0.3.25 Read the standard ↗
"Every governance system claims to act with the consent of its participants. Very few can demonstrate it. The gap between claimed consent and verifiable consent is not a philosophical distinction. It is the structural condition through which extraction operates inside systems designed to prevent it."Purpose, §1

In brief: every consent-bearing interaction made legible along two axes: who is acting, and who benefits from the action.

Information Asymmetry Classification Standard

v0.1.26 Read the standard ↗
"This standard specifies a six-class taxonomy as the precise structural answer: a framework that names the distinct information asymmetry conditions that coordination systems produce, enabling them to be detected, challenged, and structurally closed."Preamble, §1

In brief: six named classes of informational advantage, so the gap you could not name can be detected, challenged, and closed.

Structural Power Obligation Standard

v0.1.26 Read the standard ↗
"Power in coordination systems accumulates across at least three independent dimensions: where formal authority sits, who has the practical capacity to coordinate and integrate decisions, and how interpretive work is distributed across the system."Purpose, §1

In brief: power measured on three independent dimensions, so concentration becomes detectable instead of indefinitely arguable.

Regenerative Obligation Standard

v0.1.8 Read the standard ↗
"It establishes that extraction is a structural violation when it is not matched by a proportional regenerative return that meets three conditions: non-fungibility, proximity, and embeddedness in the relationship that generated the extraction obligation. It does not prohibit extraction; it specifies the conditions under which extraction is architecturally valid."Preamble, §1

In brief: what draws on contributors' capacity must return it: proportional, in kind, and inside the relationship it drew from.

Coordination Scaling Standard

v0.1.5 Read the standard ↗
"A coordination system that works at fifteen people will not work the same way at fifty. A system that works at fifty will face structural breaks at one hundred fifty that it is not designed to survive."Preamble

In brief: scale-blindness treated as a precision deficit; the thresholds named, and the conditions for crossing each one made a conformance obligation.

03The cables

Generative standards

Three cables

Sensemaking Standard

v1.1.23 Read the standard ↗
"Sensemaking is the process that produces shared language. This standard specifies what sensemaking must structurally provide in any coordination system, without prescribing method."Abstract

In brief: five structural invariants that distinguish genuinely shared language from assumed understanding, so coordination is real rather than a coincidence of motion.

Four Batteries Capacity Standard

v0.3.7 Read the standard ↗
"The capacity that coordination depends on, personal energy, relational trust, meaningful contribution, and alignment with purpose, depletes under sustained pressure without structural conditions for renewal. What gets named as individual burnout is often organizational infrastructure failure: the absence of structural conditions for regeneration."Purpose and Scope, §1

In brief: personal, relational, contribution, mission: the four stores coordination runs on, each with a charge and a developmental state the organization is obligated to condition for.

Conflict Transformation Standard

v0.2.10 Read the standard ↗
"Litigation terminates a conflict by producing a winner and a loser. Transformation converts the friction between parties into developmental signal, changes both parties, and strengthens the coordination medium rather than depleting it."Abstract

In brief: the structural preconditions under which conflict reorganizes the system instead of cycling; a companion specification covers readiness assessment before any process begins.
